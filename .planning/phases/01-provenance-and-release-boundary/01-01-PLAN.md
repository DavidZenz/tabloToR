<interfaces>
`RIGHTS.md` owns strict single-line fields `Rights-Status`, `Upstream-Repository`, `Upstream-Commit`, `Request-Status`, `Request-URL`, `Evidence-Hash`, `Reviewer`, and `Review-Date-UTC`. Allowed rights states are `blocked`, `cleared`, and `clean-room-required`.

`tools/check_release_gates.R` exposes `release_gate_main()` and accepts `--root`, `--offline`, `--assert-blocked`, and `--self-test`. `--offline` is release-readiness mode and exits nonzero for the real blocked repository. `--assert-blocked` exits zero only when every available repository artifact parses and cross-checks, `release_ready=false`, and the resulting blocker set is the documented intentional rights blocker set. `--self-test` exercises isolated temporary evidence roots.
</interfaces>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Human/public rights evidence → repository markers | Incomplete or spoofed legal evidence enters a release decision. |
| Evidence markers → process exit | A malformed record could be mistaken for an intentional block or clearance. |
| Private material → committed evidence/tests | Sensitive correspondence, credentials, or proprietary data could leak. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-001 | Elevation of privilege | rights transition | critical | mitigate | Exact states, scoped evidence, and synthetic-only eligible fixtures; every unknown transition fails. |
| T-01-002 | Tampering | `--assert-blocked` | high | mitigate | Require complete parser success and the exact intentional blocker set before returning zero. |
| T-01-003 | Spoofing | rights evidence identity | high | mitigate | Bind repository, commit, scope, URL/hash, reviewer, and date. |
| T-01-004 | Information disclosure | evidence and diagnostics | high | mitigate | Reject sensitive evidence classes and report codes without matched contents. |
| T-01-005 | Tampering | package supply chain | low | accept | No package installation occurs; use existing base/recommended R and testthat only. |
</threat_model>
