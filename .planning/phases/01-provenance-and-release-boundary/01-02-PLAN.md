<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Local draft → public upstream issue | External communication requires explicit human action. |
| Upstream response → rights state | A response may be ambiguous, incomplete, or from an unauthorized party. |
| Behavior specification → clean-room implementation | Source-derived expression must not cross into an independent implementation. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-006 | Elevation of privilege | request response transition | critical | mitigate | Draft, silence, and ambiguous responses cannot advance rights status. |
| T-01-007 | Spoofing | upstream request/response identity | high | mitigate | Bind repository, commit, URL, UTC date, content hash, responder evidence, and reviewer. |
| T-01-008 | Repudiation | external posting | high | mitigate | Blocking human action and recorded public URL/hash establish who authorized what. |
| T-01-009 | Tampering | clean-room role evidence | high | mitigate | Distinct roles, source-access attestations, provenance keys, behavior tests, and independent review. |
| T-01-010 | Information disclosure | request/specification content | high | mitigate | Exclude private correspondence, credentials, inherited excerpts, and proprietary fixtures. |
</threat_model>
